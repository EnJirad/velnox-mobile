feat: phase 2 core screens, tab navigation and settings

Expand the app into five working destinations with a settings system,
keeping the phase-2 boundary: UI and local preferences only.

Navigation
- Five-tab shell (Home, Messages, Orders, Cart, Profile) with a shared,
  Velnox-styled bottom bar that is hidden on Login and standalone screens
- Per-tab state preservation through SaveableStateHolder
- Back: settings child -> settings -> tab root -> Home -> system exit
- Settings, order detail and conversation open over the tabs

Screens
- Home: featured grid plus popular and new-arrivals rails
- Profile: identity header, stats, account shortcuts, addresses, settings icon
- Settings hub with account, preferences, security, support, legal, about and
  sign out, each opening a real child screen
- Messages: conversation list, empty state, conversation detail
- Orders: six filters, status chips, order detail with timeline
- Cart: selection, quantity steppers, removal, summary placeholders

Localisation and theme
- Thai, English and Burmese resources (277 keys each, in sync)
- DataStore-backed language and appearance preferences
- Live locale switch through a localized context, no activity recreation
- Light, dark and system appearance with complete colour schemes
- Notification toggles also stored locally

Data
- Preview content only: no prices, totals, payments or real accounts
- Every demo block carries a preview notice, so nothing reads as real business
- Ready for phase 3: signedIn is the single auth seam, no mock auth
